Fractional CTO for Startups: Technical Leadership Without the $250K Salary

Architecture decisions, hiring support, vendor management, and hands-on problem solving. 10-20 hours/week. No equity, no long-term commitment.

The Problem: You Need a CTO But Can't Afford One

You're in that growth moment: code becomes a bottleneck. Your team ships slower. Technical debt piles up. You need someone to make architecture decisions, mentor engineers, and unblock the team. But hiring a full-time CTO costs $250K+/year plus equity, and you're not sure you need that level of commitment yet.

The alternatives are worse:

  • Your founder becomes CTO: You stop fundraising, selling, or setting strategy.
  • You hire a junior as tech lead: Good engineer, but no vision. Decisions happen in silos.
  • You use agencies: Fast, but they optimize for hours, not your long-term success. They leave when the contract ends.
  • You do nothing: Code slows you down. Your best engineers leave. Investors see the risk.

What a Fractional CTO Does (10-20 Hours/Week)

Architecture & Technical Direction

  • Evaluate new tools: Database decisions, frontend frameworks, deploy platforms. I give you a written recommendation with trade-offs.
  • Refactoring roadmap: What technical debt is killing you. What can wait. What to tackle first.
  • Scaling decisions: When to hire. What skills to hire. Build vs. buy.

Hiring Support

  • Define the role: What you actually need (and what you don't).
  • Interview candidates: Technical questions, architecture conversations, cultural fit.
  • Onboarding: First 2 weeks with new engineers, unblocking them fast.

Hands-On Problem Solving

  • Unblock stuck projects: Performance bottlenecks, deploy problems, security issues.
  • Code reviews: Strategic feedback on critical paths, not every PR.
  • Crisis management: Outages, security incidents, "how do we fix this fast?"

Vendor & Tool Management

  • Negotiate contracts: AWS, Stripe, cloud platforms. Save 20-40% with the right structure.
  • Monitor costs: Where are you overspending? What tools pay for themselves?
  • Partnership roadmap: Which integrations matter. What comes next.

FAQ

How is this different from an agency?

Agencies send different people each project. I'm your CTO: continuity, context, long-term thinking. I care about technical debt because I have to live with it.

Can you help with hiring?

Yes. I define the role, interview candidates, review code samples, help with the first weeks of onboarding.

What if I need more than 20 hours/week?

We scale. Or if it's temporary (a crisis, a big project), we do project-based work on top of the retainer.

What technology stacks do you handle?

Laravel, PHP, Symfony, React, Next.js, Node.js, NestJS, Angular, Java Spring, Python, Firebase, AWS, GCP, Azure, databases, testing frameworks. If it's not on the list, I learn fast.

Can you start now?

Usually yes. Let's talk first about your situation: your team, your growth plans, your bottlenecks. I'll give you honest feedback on whether a fractional CTO is the right fit.

Do you need to be hands-on in the code?

Sometimes. For architecture decisions, hiring, vendor management: no. For unblocking critical issues, refactoring bottlenecks, security: yes, I code.
