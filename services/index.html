FAQ

Common questions

How do you work? Fixed scope, hourly, fractional CTO?

All three. Most projects are fixed scope: one deliverable, one price, timeline in writing, scope in writing. For ongoing needs — code reviews, architecture work, hiring — I offer hourly or fractional CTO roles. I bill in 4-hour blocks minimum. For fractional CTO, expect 10–20 hours per week on retainer.

Where are you? What time zone?

I'm in Cuenca, Ecuador (UTC-5) — the same timezone as US Eastern. I work 9 AM–6 PM local time, which overlaps fully with US business hours. I prefer async communication: written Slack messages, GitHub issues, detailed PRs. Meetings are the exception, not the rule.

What does the 2-week audit deliver?

A risk map of your system: architecture strengths and weaknesses, security findings, technical debt hotspots, and a prioritized roadmap. You get a document (10–20 pages), a walkthrough, and a clear picture of what to fix first. Fixed scope, fixed price. The fee is credited toward follow-up work if we continue.

Can you take over code written by someone else?

Yes. That's where project rescue comes in. I've inherited half-built platforms, abandoned codebases, and undocumented systems. My first step: review the code, identify what works, map the risks, and decide what to rewrite vs. refactor. Once I understand the system, I can own it, improve it, and help your team maintain it.

Do you do ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Most of my clients continue with me on retainer or hourly basis after the initial build. That might be bug fixes and feature releases, code reviews, architecture decisions, or fractional CTO leadership. We define the scope at the start and adjust as things change.
