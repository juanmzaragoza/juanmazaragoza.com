Google My Business → Looker Studio

Custom community connector built with Apps Script to automate GMB data integration and dashboard creation for a Barcelona marketing agency

TechJavaScript
TechApps Script
TechLooker Studio
TechGMB API

When Marcos from New Condal approached me, he had a clear challenge: seamlessly integrating data from multiple Google My Business accounts into his marketing dashboards. As a digital marketing agency in Barcelona, manually connecting and analyzing GMB data for multiple clients was time-consuming and inefficient.

Solution

I developed a custom community connector using Apps Script. This solution bridged the gap between Google My Business accounts and Looker Studio dashboards, enabling Marcos to access key business data effortlessly — reviews, ratings, visibility metrics, and more.

The connector automated the extraction and organization of essential GMB information, making it readily available for analysis and reporting without any manual effort.

Dashboard Design

Once the data was flowing seamlessly, I collaborated with Marcos to design visually engaging and informative dashboards. These dashboards transformed raw data into meaningful insights, displayed in a way that was both aesthetically pleasing and effective for communicating results to New Condal's clients.

Outcome

The solution eliminated manual labor in pulling reports, allowed the agency to focus on what truly matters — helping their clients grow — and elevated New Condal's services by giving clients a clearer understanding of their online presence and marketing opportunities.
