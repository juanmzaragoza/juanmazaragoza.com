Real Estate Chatbot — Casa Domo Peru

Automated client interactions, site visit scheduling, and lead management using Manychat and Google Sheets

TechJavaScript
TechManychat
TechGoogle Sheets

Casa Domo · Real Estate · Peru · Developer · Jan 2025

Results

  • Automated significant portion of client interaction process
  • Lead management and scheduling streamlined
  • Team engaged more effectively with potential buyers

For Nicole at Casa Domo in Peru, I developed a chatbot solution to assist her team in scheduling visits to various real estate projects where they offer land for sale. The entire solution was built using Manychat and Google Sheets.

Features

  • Identifies any type of message sent by the customer
  • Recognizes when a client wants to know more about a specific project
  • Avoids duplicate greetings or repetitive interactions
  • Sends predefined messages tailored for each project — consistent and accurate
  • Provides generic responses with a friendly, human-like tone
  • Uses data stored in a Google Sheet to provide real-time answers
  • Helps clients schedule a visit to view the land
  • Records both scheduled and unscheduled visits in Google Sheets for follow-ups

Outcome

Automated a significant portion of the client interaction process, making it easier to manage leads and improve efficiency. The tool empowered the team to engage more effectively with potential buyers while saving valuable time and resources.
