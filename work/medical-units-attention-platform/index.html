Medical Attention Platform — Buenos Aires Gov

Technical Director for a pharmacy tracking system for the City of Buenos Aires — Angular + Java, agile methodology, end-to-end delivery

TechAngular
TechJava
TechMariaDB
TechAgile

Served as Technical Director for a comprehensive tracking system for pharmacies dispensing medicines on behalf of the Government of the City of Buenos Aires, ensuring full traceability of medicine distribution operations.

System Objectives

  • Monitor pharmacies dispensing government-subsidized medicines
  • Manage subsidies and analyze statistics on most needed medicine types
  • Implement preventive measures for supply chain continuity
  • Ensure full traceability of all distribution operations

Technical Leadership

Selected and implemented the technology stack: Angular (frontend), Java (backend), MariaDB (database). Collaborated closely with analysts and the Product Owner to define business rules. Led the team using agile development methodologies, ensuring adherence to deadlines while delivering a high-quality product. Instituted robust code delivery practices for independent QA testing.
