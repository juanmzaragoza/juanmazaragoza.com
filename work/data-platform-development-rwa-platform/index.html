Contributed to the RWA Platform Project over a three-week period, playing a pivotal role in developing a solution based on Next.js. Using detailed Figma mockups as the blueprint, helped design and build a web application that integrates data from various sources into a BigQuery data warehouse.

Demonstrated the ability to replicate complex Figma mockups accurately, integrate advanced technologies like BigQuery and Flask, and leverage modern web tools to enhance business intelligence and facilitate data-driven decision-making.
