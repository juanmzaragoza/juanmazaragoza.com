WordPress E-Commerce — Amedia Social

Resolved critical WooCommerce issues: cart calculation bugs and payment error messaging for a social media services platform

Amedia Social · SaaS · Brazil · Developer · Nov 2024

Results

  • Cart calculations restored to accuracy
  • Payment error messages clarified for users
  • Checkout abandonment reduced

Amedia Social, managed by Simbiotic Digital, approached me with two critical issues affecting their WooCommerce-based platform designed to boost social media performance and online reputation.

Issue 1 — Cart Calculation Bug

Users were experiencing incorrect total calculations whenever they selected product variations. After diving into the codebase, I discovered the problem stemmed from a JavaScript script responsible for dynamically calculating cart totals. A recent WooCommerce plugin update had altered the DOM structure of the cart page, leaving the script outdated.

By analyzing the new DOM structure and rewriting the script accordingly, I restored accurate pricing calculations, ensuring that users could confidently proceed with their purchases.

Issue 2 — Payment Error Messaging

When customers attempted to pay and the transaction failed, the error messages were vague and unhelpful, leading to frustration and abandonment. I implemented a solution using WooCommerce's woocommerce_add_error hook, creating a function to replace default messages with more descriptive, user-friendly explanations.

Outcome

Accurate cart calculations restored trust in the shopping experience, while clearer error messaging contributed to smoother customer interactions and reduced abandonment on the checkout page.
