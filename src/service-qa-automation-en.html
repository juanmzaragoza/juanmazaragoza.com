QA Automation & Release Confidence

Automated tests that check critical flows before and after every deploy — so your team ships with confidence, not anxiety.

The problem

Manual QA doesn't scale. When releases depend on engineers clicking through screens, bugs slip through on the flows that cost you the most: purchases, permissions, payments. The bigger the platform, the worse this gets.

What I do

  • Testing strategy from scratch: what to test, at which layer, and in what order
  • End-to-end tests with Playwright (3+ browsers, mobile viewports)
  • Backend integration tests with Pest or PHPUnit for Laravel
  • Post-deploy quality gates: critical flows run automatically on every push; a failed test stops the deploy
  • Payment gateway testing — Stripe, MercadoPago, 3DS flows

Who it's for

SaaS teams with no test coverage (or tests nobody runs), Laravel projects moving toward CI/CD, and platforms where a failed release has real cost in revenue or reputation.

Results from real projects

  • 20 → 101 end-to-end tests on a high-volume ticketing platform in Spain
  • 616 backend integration test cases
  • Release validation from days to minutes
